About SOLROLL Attendance dApp

Overview

SOLROLL Attendance dApp is designed to make attendance management simple and transparent using blockchain technology. Teachers can mark attendance on the Solana network, and students can verify their records anytime.

Mission

Our mission is to provide educational institutions with a secure, reliable, and modern attendance system that leverages decentralized technologies.

Learning Purpose

This project was created as a learning exercise to understand how blockchain, web development, and decentralized applications (dApps) work. It demonstrates connecting a wallet, sending transactions, and storing data on Solana while practicing frontend and JavaScript skills.
