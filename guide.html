SOLROL Attendance dApp Guide

Introduction

SOLROL Attendance dApp is a learning project created to understand blockchain-based attendance management using Solana. It allows teachers to mark attendance and students to view their records securely.

1. Login

Open the Login page, enter your name, and select your role (Teacher or Student). Click Continue to proceed to the Home page.

2. Home Page

  • Click Connect Wallet if you are a teacher to connect your Phantom wallet.
  • The wallet address will be displayed partially once connected.
  • Your role button will become active. Click it to go to the Teacher or Student Dashboard.

3. Teacher Dashboard

  • Select a student from the dropdown list.
  • Choose Present or Absent using the radio buttons.
  • Click Mark Attendance to save the record.
  • The attendance is stored locally and a transaction is sent to Solana Devnet (for learning purposes).
  • All records appear in the attendance list at the bottom.

4. Student Dashboard

  • View your attendance history in a table with Date, Transaction ID, and Status.
  • If no records exist yet, the table will show "No records yet".
  • Click Back to Home to return.

5. Notes & Learning Purpose

  • This project is for learning and educational purposes, not commercial use.
  • Teachers must have Phantom Wallet installed to record attendance.
  • Solana Devnet is used to demonstrate blockchain transactions safely.
